% Design, Fig. D (Section 3.4): host-memory sector execution and the exchange at the window boundary, on node 1 of the
% running example (node 1 owns sectors 01 and 11 of W_k). (1) GPU 1 loads sector 11, a list of fixed-size chunks in
% host memory; (2) it applies the gates of W_k (Fig. B); (3) when the output outgrows the buffer, the sector is split by
% one more row of the kernel of W_k (x2) and both halves continue; (4) the output is split by the rows of W_k+1 (x0, z2):
% sector 01' stays on node 1 and is appended to its chunk list, sectors 00' and 10' belong to node 0; (5) at the window
% boundary the Sector Exchanger sends them over MPI in rounds of one chunk per sector, and the sent chunks return to the
% pool.
