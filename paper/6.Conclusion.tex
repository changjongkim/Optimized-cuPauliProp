\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% Three sentences, 85-120 words, no future work.
We present \SectorQ, a distributed Pauli propagation system that derives conserved parity sectors from the generators of each gate window and confines merge and truncation to sector execution within that window.
The runtime selects conserved rows using sampled live terms during GPU execution and stores sectors in distributed host memory, with term redistribution at window boundaries and further sector subdivision when device buffers are insufficient.
Across seven circuits, \SectorQ reduces simulation time relative to original-circuit \cuPP by 4.4-9.7$\times$ at the largest common settings on 16 GPUs and completes a 140$\times$ larger term population on 512 GPUs.
