% Where communication happens (Section 3.3), second style (the look of the ScaleQsim paper).
% What the figure shows: the same circuit and the same four GPUs under the two schemes (cuPauliProp applies its 5,420 gates, SectorQ the 2,540 rotations left by the Clifford frame).
%   Left:  cuPauliProp's distributed mode. Every gate is followed by a global step (red mark after every gate
%          cell): all GPUs merge and redistribute their terms, 22 collective calls per gate on every GPU,
%          because a new term may belong to any GPU.
%   Right: SectorQ. The gates are grouped into 20 windows of 127 gates; every GPU holds one sector and applies
%          the whole window to it; terms are exchanged only at the window boundaries (blue marks), cut where
%          the terms are fewest (159 million of the 1.36 billion terms of the peak move).
%   Bottom: the measured cost of the two schemes (first three rows: kicked Ising, 22.5 million terms, 4 GPUs;
%          last row: kicked Ising, cutoff 1e-5, 16 GPUs).
% Message (one sentence): the existing multi-GPU implementation coordinates all GPUs after every gate; SectorQ
% applies a whole window of gates inside each GPU and exchanges terms once, at the window boundary.
% Colors: peach = a gate, gray = a term, orange with a red outline = a new term, blue/green/yellow/violet = the
% four sectors, teal = the step that moves terms between GPUs, red = the cost that SectorQ removes,
% blue = a window boundary.
