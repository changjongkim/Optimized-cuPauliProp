% Motivation (slide 2 of the deck), second style (the look of the ScaleQsim paper), drawn with pictures.
% What the figure shows, for a reader who does not know the field:
%   Left:   the same term (Pauli string X Z Y I) lies in two pieces on two GPUs, sizes 0.7 and 0.6 (in units of
%           1e-4); the cutoff is 1.0. (1) If each GPU decides alone, both pieces are below the cutoff, both are
%           dropped, the term is lost and the answer is wrong. (2) If the pieces are merged first, the term has
%           size 1.3, which is above the cutoff, and it is kept.
%           Measured without a merge across GPUs: 0.1675 on 4 GPUs and 0.189 on 16 GPUs, the correct value is 0.1531.
%   Right:  the two existing ways to merge across GPUs, and what each costs.
%           (1) cuPauliProp's distributed mode: all GPUs stop and merge after every gate (22 calls to all GPUs
%               per gate); the same job takes 2.9 s on 1 GPU and 71 s on 16 GPUs. Price: time.
%           (2) Merging only before a truncation: the unmerged copies pile up in GPU memory until it is full;
%               2 of the 7 circuits run out of memory on 16 GPUs. Price: memory.
%   Bottom: what is needed (all pieces of a term on the same GPU) and what SectorQ does.
% Message (one sentence): when the list is spread over GPUs, the same term lies in pieces on different GPUs: each
% piece alone looks too small and is thrown away, so the GPUs must merge first, and the two existing ways to do
% that cost either time or memory.
% Visual language (shared with design2_background.tex): a term is a vertical bar on a baseline, its height is the
% size of its coefficient, its color is its Pauli string, its label stands under it; the cutoff is a dashed red
% line with the word "Cutoff" at its end; a dropped term is a faded bar (gray label) with a red cross; a merged
% term shows its two parts by a thin dashed line.
% The sizes 0.7 and 0.6 are an illustration (the cutoff is that of the measured setting); every other number is
% measured (the sources of the figures deck/n03 and deck/n07 cite the logs).
% Colors: blue bar = the term X Z Y I; in the memory picture one color per term, hatched = an unmerged copy;
% khaki = a GPU, teal = the step in which all GPUs take part, yellow = an action or a price, green = the good outcome;
% red = the cutoff, the dropped pieces, the lost term, the calls between GPUs, the 16-GPU time, "Full", "2 of 7".
% Canvas: 17.6 cm x 8.95 cm. Bars: 0.44 cm wide, 1.38 cm high per unit of size (the same scale in all three
% bar pictures); time bars: 2.75 cm for 71 s, 0.112 cm for 2.9 s. No text is smaller than \footnotesize.
% Each numbered box is drawn in a scope whose origin is its top left corner.
