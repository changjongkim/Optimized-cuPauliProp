% Background of SectorQ (slide "Background 2: Pauli propagation tracks a list of terms"), second style (the look
% of the ScaleQsim paper). Drawn with pictures instead of tables, for a reader who has never seen the subject.
% Visual language (shared with design2_motivation.tex):
%   a term     = a vertical bar; its height is the size of its coefficient, its color is its Pauli string, the
%                string stands under it
%   the cutoff = a dashed red horizontal line across a row of bars
%   a dropped term = its bar faded, with a red cross
%   the list of terms = a row of such bars on a thin baseline
% What the figure shows:
%   left    what is kept: a circuit (127 qubits) and, from it, a list of terms (four bars)
%   middle  the three things every gate does to the list: (1) branch, one term becomes two; (2) merge, two terms
%           with the same label are added into one (0.20 + 0.15 = 0.35); (3) truncate, the term below the cutoff
%           is dropped
%   right   how long the list gets, as a unit picture: one small khaki square = what one GPU holds (about 100
%           million terms). Terms kept by the 127-qubit kicked Ising circuit at three cutoffs: 22.5 million = a
%           quarter of one square, 1.4 billion = 14 squares, 100.1 billion = 1,000 squares (50 x 20). The square has
%           the same size in the legend and in all three rows, so the areas compare.
%           Below: the error of a 30-qubit test at the cutoffs 1e-3 and 1e-7 (0.019 and 0.00007).
%   bottom  the chain that follows: lower cutoff -> more terms kept -> more accurate answer -> more memory and time
% Message (one sentence): Pauli propagation keeps a list of terms; every gate makes the list longer, equal terms are
% added together, small ones are thrown away, and the list still grows far beyond what one GPU can hold.
% Colors: one color per Pauli string (Z, Z I blue; Y, Y I green; Z X yellow; X Z violet), orange = a rotation gate,
% hatched = a Clifford gate, khaki square = what one GPU holds, pale yellow = the conclusion, red = the cutoff,
% the dropped term and the number of terms one GPU holds.
% Bar heights are schematic except in Merge, where 0.20, 0.15 and 0.35 are drawn to scale.
% Canvas: 17.6 cm x 8.92 cm; no text is smaller than \footnotesize.
% The boxes of the middle and right panels are drawn in scopes whose origin is the top left corner of the box.
% Block arrows are placed by their tip.
